How are RFM values and scores calculated?
  • Recency: days from the customer's latest transaction to the analysis date (lower is better).
  • Frequency: the number of transactions made by the customer (all rows with the same Customer_ID).
  • Monetary: the total amount the customer spent.
  • Scores: customers are ranked and split into five groups, 1 to 5. For Recency the most recent customers score 5. Customers with identical values always get the same score, so the groups are not always exactly equal in size.
  • RFM is a way of describing customer behaviour. It is not the machine-learning step: the clustering module uses these three values as its input.
